MirrorVO

Mirror your iPhone screen to your TV

Mirrors your iPhone wirelessly to a Chromecast TV, to the web browser of a TV without Chromecast, or to another iPhone. With sound, up to 1080p.

LayoutVO

Plan your room in 3D, place furniture

Enter or scan your room size, drag and drop furniture, walk through it in 3D and see it in your room with AR. Send a scaled plan and a materials list to your contractor.
